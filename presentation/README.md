# Learning within reach

Open `index.html` directly in a browser. The 13-slide HTML/JS presentation works offline and embeds its images, styles, scripts, and speaker notes. No installation or build step is required. Slide 2 tells our story: Frank’s work for a student-run NGO supporting education in rural China, and our wish to create a great product for our own kids.

## Change the wording in your browser

1. Click **Edit text** in the bottom toolbar.
2. Click a heading, paragraph, table cell, or label and type your changes. Normal keyboard shortcuts for selecting text and undoing edits work while the text has focus.
3. Use the slide buttons to move between slides while editing.
4. Click **Download** to save an updated `learning-within-reach.html` file. Open that downloaded file to present or continue editing it.

**Done editing** returns to presentation mode. It does not save the file. Changes in the open browser tab are temporary until you download a copy. The browser does not overwrite the original `index.html`. To update the project copy, replace `presentation/index.html` with the downloaded HTML.

This mode edits visible wording. For new slides, layout changes, or speaker notes, ask Codex for the change or edit the corresponding `<section class="slide ...">` and `<template class="notes">` in the HTML. Each section has a searchable `data-title`. Slide navigation and overview counts update automatically. Keep text short enough for its layout and review the slide after editing. Changing visible facts does not automatically update the notes or source citations.

## Present

Slide 4 ("A word becomes a conversation") includes **Watch video demo**, which opens [the YouTube demo](https://youtu.be/ZAaAPILTohk) in a new tab. The short URL is `https://youtu.be/ZAaAPILTohk`. The presentation works offline; playing the video requires an internet connection.

Controls:

- Left / right arrows or Space: navigate. Shift+Space goes back.
- O: slide overview. N: speaker notes and source links.
- F: fullscreen. Escape: close a dialog. Home / End: first / last slide.
- Swipe horizontally on a touch screen.
- Browser Print: print all slides or save to PDF, with backgrounds enabled.
